# Data

All data is synthetic, generated by physiological and physical models from
seeds. There is no patient data, no personal data, and nothing derived from
a clinical database. Ranges below are the sampling distributions of the
hidden cases; the code is the authority.

## Generation

* A case is a pure function of one 128-bit seed. `streams(seed, n)` spawns
  independent generators through `numpy.random.SeedSequence`, one per source
  of variation (patient, meals, faults, noise), so adding a source never
  changes the others.
* Rejection sampling keeps only cases where the task is solvable with margin
  (t02: a feasible regimen with `d_min <= 0.85 d_max`; t04: a robust plan
  with a power window of at least 5 %).
* Splits: `tests/dev_seeds.txt` (public, 8 per task; development only) and
  `private_seeds.txt` (generated with `bioeng seeds new`, never committed,
  `.gitignore`d) for held-out evaluation. Validation-campaign seeds are
  derived from a fixed master seed and the probe name.

## Distributions

### t01 ECG
McSharry model, 360 Hz, 60 s. Heart rate, rhythm (respiratory sinus
arrhythmia, Mayer waves, AR component), wave angles/amplitudes/widths and
overall amplitude vary per case around McSharry et al. (2003) Table 1.
Contaminants: baseline wander 0.05-0.40 Hz, mains near 60 Hz (+-0.05 Hz),
white electrode noise.

### t02 Pharmacokinetics
CL 3-7 L/h, V1 15-30 L, Q 5-15 L/h, V2 20-50 L (log-uniform), 1500 mg
over 1 h at t = 0, 14 samples 1-48 h, log-normal proportional error
sigma = 0.10. Window 10-40 mg/L, targets 11.5-35 mg/L, intervals 6/8/12/24 h.

### t03 Glucose-insulin
Body mass 55-95 kg, p1 0.002-0.008 1/min, p2 0.02-0.03 1/min, S_I 3e-4 to
8e-4, n 0.09-0.15 1/min, Gb 105-135 mg/dL, basal 0.0085 / S_I x (mass/70)
x U(0.8, 1.25) mU/min, sensor lag 5-12 min. Meals 30-60 g near 07:00, 12:30,
19:00 (+-30 min), absorption peak 40-60 min. Exercise 45 min starting
15:30-16:30 (the day starts at 06:00), uptake 0.004-0.008 1/min, insulin-action
gain 0.5-1.0. Faults: 30 min dropout, +60-90 mg/dL spike (15 min),
-40-60 mg/dL compression (20 min).

### t04 Bioheat
Liver-like tissue: k 0.52 W/(m K), rho 1060 kg/m^3, c 3600 J/(kg K), blood
rho_b c_b 3.86e6 J/(m^3 K). Source width 5-9 mm, tumour radius 4-8 mm,
nominal perfusion 0.001-0.006 1/s with +-20 % uncertainty. Heating menu
180-1200 s, 300 s cooling, 100 C limit, 5 mm margin.

### t05 PK debugging
CL 50-120 mL/min, V1 10-25 L, Q 5-20 L/h, V2 20-60 L; five regimen
families per case (standard, short late infusion, bolus, 14-day, coincident
doses), amounts and times varied per case.

## Limitations

* Synthetic physiology is simpler than real patients: no arrhythmias or
  motion artefacts (t01), no inter-occasion variability (t02), no glucagon,
  stress or illness (t03), no large vessels or anisotropy (t04).
* The models are standard and cited, but a task passed here is evidence of
  engineering skill on the model, not of clinical safety.
* Not a medical device. Not for clinical use.
